## Before Contacting Support

When you email support, please include:

- Aura Space app version.
- iOS version.
- iPhone model.
- The exact page or tool you were using.
- The message, error, or screenshot you saw.
- Whether the issue happens again and the steps that reproduce it.

If the issue involves a purchase or subscription, do not send your Apple ID password, payment-card information, or full receipt screenshots. Apple handles subscription payments and billing.

## Subscription and Restore Purchases

Aura Space Pro is an App Store auto-renewable subscription that unlocks the current Pro tool pages in the app. Apple displays and handles pricing, free-trial eligibility, renewal, billing, cancellation, and refunds.

Common actions:

- View the paywall: open Aura Space > Settings > Aura Pro, or tap a locked Pro tool.
- Restore purchases: open Aura Space > Settings > Aura Pro > Restore Purchases.
- Manage or cancel a subscription: open iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, then choose Aura Space.
- Trial eligibility: Apple determines introductory offer eligibility. Seeing a subscription entry in the app does not mean every Apple ID is eligible for a free trial.

If Pro is not active:

1. Confirm that the device is signed in with the Apple ID that purchased the subscription.
2. Confirm that the network connection is available.
3. Tap Restore Purchases in Aura Space.
4. Check Aura Space Pro status in App Store account subscriptions.
5. If Pro still does not restore, contact support with the purchase time, subscription type, and current app version. Do not send full payment-card information.

## Permission Issues

Aura Space requests permissions only when you open a related tool. You can change permissions in iOS Settings:

1. Open iPhone Settings.
2. Find Aura Space.
3. Review camera, photos, microphone, location, motion, notifications, Health, NFC, or network-related permissions.
4. Return to Aura Space and open the tool again.

Common permission mapping:

| Feature | May require |
| --- | --- |
| QR scanning and colour picker | Camera |
| Saving images or exported assets | Photos |
| Sound meter | Microphone |
| Compass, speed, and altitude | Location, motion, or device sensors |
| Accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and barometer | Device hardware and motion/sensor capabilities |
| NFC | A supported physical iPhone and user-initiated NFC read/write |
| Activity summary | Apple Health authorization |
| Focus timer and status updates | Notifications or Live Activity, depending on the feature |

The iOS Simulator cannot fully verify NFC, camera quality, some sensors, notification delivery, or background behavior. Use a physical device when possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Aura Space require an account?

No. Aura Space does not create an account and does not show ads.

### Does Aura Space upload my data?

Tool history and settings are stored primarily on your device. Requests leave the device only when you choose to export or share content, or when you use a tool that requires an external service, such as AI API usage checks, IP lookup, network speed tests, DNS/tunnel setup, or App Store subscription verification.

### Why does AI usage not show data?

AI usage depends on the provider you configure, API key type, account permissions, regional support, and the provider's public API. A regular consumer subscription does not always include API usage or billing query permissions. Confirm that the API key belongs to the correct account and that the provider supports the requested query.

### Why are network speed or IP lookup results unstable?

Network speed and IP lookup can be affected by your carrier, VPN, proxy, routing, speed-test endpoint, geolocation database, captive portal, and temporary network conditions. Results are best-effort references for the current network environment.

### Can Ad Shield block every ad?

No guarantee. Ad Shield depends on iOS DNS, Safari content blocker, tunnel settings, rule lists, the selected DNS service, and system policy. Aura Space does not decrypt HTTPS traffic and does not promise to block 100% of ads.

### Why do widgets or Live Activities not refresh in real time?

iOS manages widget and Live Activity refresh frequency. Widgets are not continuously running real-time monitors. Confirm that your iOS version supports the feature and open Aura Space to refresh shared state.

### Can Aura Space automatically set wallpapers or replace system icons?

No. Aura Space cannot silently set or rotate system wallpapers, cannot silently replace system icons, and cannot replace the Lock Screen, StandBy, or system Dynamic Island behavior.

### What should I do if file, audio, or document conversion fails?

Confirm that the file format is supported by the current version, that the file is not damaged, and that you kept the original file. Conversion, archive management, and long screenshot stitching depend on user-selected files, iOS file access, and format differences.
